# Product Guardrails

Anchor's commercial model uses aggregate attendance and finance data to help school systems understand funding exposure. That must never turn into assigning monetary worth to individual students.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. No student-level screen displays a dollar value.
2. Student case priority may use need, safety, urgency, actionability, and intervention evidence—not funding weight or modeled revenue.
3. Finance views remain aggregate at campus, network, district, or approved cohort level.
4. Anchor never alters attendance records to improve reported funding.
5. Modeled gross formula value is always labeled separately from verified net funding impact.
6. Safety, discipline, clinical, and high-impact decisions remain under authorized human control.
7. Synthetic data is used in development until production privacy/security controls are ready.
8. Student data is never sold or used for advertising.
9. Every finance-relevant workflow eventually requires an auditable event trail.
10. District/state attendance-accounting rules override simplified MVP calculations.
